## Full description (max 4000 characters)

Veritas Tennis is the tennis companion for players at Veritas University, Abuja. Keep score on court, track your progress, climb the campus ladder and run your own tournaments, all from your phone.

🎾 LIVE SCOREKEEPER
• Tap a side to give them the point. The app handles 15-30-40, deuce and advantage, no-ad scoring, 7-point tiebreaks, 10-point match tiebreaks and short sets.
• Shows who is serving (including doubles rotation), which side to serve from, when to change ends, and when it is break, set or match point.
• Record aces, double faults, winners and errors, and undo any point.
• The screen stays on while you score, and a match in progress is saved even if you close the app.

📊 STATS AND HISTORY
• Every match is saved with full stats: points won, service points, break points and more.
• See your win rate, streaks, sets, tiebreaks and head-to-head record against every opponent.
• Already played? Log the score in seconds (for example 6-4 3-6 7-6).
• Share results straight to WhatsApp.

🏆 CAMPUS LADDER
• Everyone starts at 1200. Win ranked singles matches to climb, and earn more for beating stronger players.
• Player profiles with rating history charts, recent form, and Call and WhatsApp buttons to arrange your next match.

🏟️ TOURNAMENTS
• Run hall, departmental or club events as a knockout (seeded, with byes) or a round robin with a full standings table.
• Score fixtures live or enter results, and winners move on automatically.
• Share the draw and results with all players in one tap.

👥 CLUB SYNC
• Create a club and share its code in your WhatsApp group. Every member then sees the same players, results, ladder and tournaments.
• Works offline and syncs when you are connected. Your practice log stays private.

💪 TRAINING
• 15 drills with step-by-step instructions and coaching tips: warm-ups, groundstrokes, serve and return, net play, footwork and match play.
• Practice log with weekly totals and an activity streak.
• Rules guide: scoring, tiebreaks, serving, doubles, line calls, court etiquette and playing safely in the heat.

No sign-up, no adverts and no tracking. Your data stays on your phone unless you join a club.
