# Veritas Tennis — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 10 October 2026_

Veritas Tennis is a tennis app for players at Veritas University, Abuja. This
policy explains what the app stores and shares.

## Data stored on your phone

Everything you enter is saved on your own device: your player profile, the
players you add (name, department or level, playing level, and optionally a
phone number and notes), match results and statistics, tournaments, and your
practice log. It stays on your phone unless you choose one of the options
below.

## Club sync (optional)

If you **create or join a club**, the app sends your club's players, match
results and tournaments to our server so that every member of the club sees
the same ladder and results. This can include the names, departments, levels,
phone numbers and notes entered for players.

- Your **practice log is never uploaded**.
- Club data is stored on [Supabase](https://supabase.com), our hosting
  provider, in the United Kingdom (London region), and is sent over encrypted
  (HTTPS) connections.
- Club data can only be read or changed with that club's **club code**. Anyone
  you give the code to can see and edit the club's results, so share it only
  with your club.
- We do not sell the data, show adverts, or use it for anything other than
  running the club sync feature.

## Sharing and backups

When you use **Share**, **Back up**, **Call** or **WhatsApp**, the app hands the
text, file or phone number to the app you choose (for example WhatsApp or
Google Drive). Those apps' own privacy policies then apply.

## What we do not collect

The app has no accounts or sign-in, no advertising, no analytics or tracking,
and does not access your location, contacts, camera or microphone.

## Children

The app is intended for university students and staff aged 18 and over.

## Deleting your data

- **On your phone:** delete players, matches, tournaments or practice sessions
  in the app, or use **Settings › Erase all data**. Uninstalling the app also
  removes everything stored on the phone.
- **Club data:** deleting a player, match or tournament while you are in a club
  removes it for the whole club. To have a whole club's data deleted from the
  server, contact us using the details below and include the club code.
